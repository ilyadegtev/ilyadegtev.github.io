About me

Experimental condensed matter physicist with a PhD focused on quantum materials and strongly correlated systems. My research combines materials synthesis, surface science, spectroscopy, microscopy, and theoretical modelling, with particular interests in high-Tc superconductivity, correlated electron systems, and atomic-scale surface reactions.

I have worked in academic and industrial research environments and developed a broad, interdisciplinary approach to experimental physics, from designing new experimental methods to analysing and modelling complex physical systems.

Current research interests

  • Quantum materials and high Tc superconductivity
  • Strongly correlated electron systems
  • Surface science and atomic-scale reactions
  • Thin films, 2D materials and overlayer structures
