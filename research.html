PhD Thesis

    My PhD research focused on the synthesis and characterization of two-dimensional (2D) silver fluorides as quantum materials analogous to cuprates. I developed a method to generate highly reactive monoatomic fluorine and built a custom UHV apparatus to precisely control surface reactions, enabling the growth of flat fluorine overlayers on Ag surfaces. Based on LT STM data, a simple phase diagram of fluorine on Ag was reconstructed. Complementary XAS and RIXS studies on bulk AgF₂, supported by DFT and analytical computations, revealed strong parallels with cuprates and established silver fluorides as a promising platform for correlated-electron physics.

    Expertise: Quantum materials and high-Tc superconductivity; Surface reactions at the atomic scale; data analysis and mathematical modelling.
    Experimental techniques: UHV, PLD, LT STM, XAS, RIXS.
    Theory: Strongly correlated materials, Density Functional Theory (DFT).
    Results: three articles published, one international patent, six months of industrial experience,
