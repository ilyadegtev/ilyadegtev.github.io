Brief CV

Driven by my longstanding interest in physics and mathematics, I pursued my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at the Department of Physics, Lomonosov Moscow State University, specializing in condensed matter. However, because of my diverse interests, I changed my career path; after a couple of years in data analysis, I worked as a freelance photographer and civil engineer. Ultimately, these experiences helped me clarify my long-term goals and strengthened my motivation to return to academic research. I resumed my scientific career by undertaking doctoral studies in Physics at Sapienza University of Rome. This research led to my PhD thesis, "Towards Silver Fluoride Quantum Materials", which I successfully defended in September 2025.
